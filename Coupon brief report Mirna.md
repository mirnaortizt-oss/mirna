# Coupon Acceptance Analysis: Customer Insights

## Overview
This project analyzes survey data to understand the key differences between drivers who accept mobile driving coupons and those who ignore them. By looking at a driver's destination, current time, passengers, and past habits, we can identify exactly what makes a promotional offer successful. 

## Key Differences: Who Accepts vs. Who Rejects

Overall, the data shows that a customer's **preexisting habits** and their **immediate driving context** are the biggest factors in deciding whether to accept a coupon. People rarely change their routine for a coupon, but they will gladly use one if it fits into what they already like to do.

### 1. Bar Coupons
* **The Customer Who Accepts:** This driver already goes to bars at least once a month. They are typically younger adults, or adults over 25 who are driving alone or with friends. 
* **The Customer Who Rejects:** Drivers who have children in the car almost universally reject bar coupons. Additionally, older drivers and people who rarely visit bars are highly unlikely to take a detour for a drink, regardless of the discount.

### 2. Coffee House Coupons
* **The Customer Who Accepts:** Timing is everything for this group. Drivers are highly likely to accept coffee coupons if they are on the road mid-morning (10 AM) or early afternoon (2 PM). Like the bar group, these customers already have a habit of visiting coffee shops multiple times a month.
* **The Customer Who Rejects:** Drivers on the road early in the morning or late at night tend to ignore these offers. People who do not typically spend money at coffee houses are also unlikely to be swayed by a coupon.

### 3. Inexpensive Restaurants (Under $20)
* **The Customer Who Accepts:** Drivers who already frequent inexpensive restaurants (more than 4 times a month) and earn an income under $50,000 show a strong likelihood of accepting these food coupons.

## Main Takeaways and Recommendations
Coupons are most effective when they act as a reward for existing behavior rather than an incentive to try something entirely new. 

To maximize coupon acceptance rates, businesses should target their digital marketing based on:
1. **Past Behavior:** Only send venue-specific coupons to drivers who already have a documented history of visiting those types of establishments. 
2. **Time and Context:** Time coffee promotions for the mid-morning slump (10 AM) and actively avoid sending nightlife/bar coupons to drivers who are known to be traveling with young passengers.
